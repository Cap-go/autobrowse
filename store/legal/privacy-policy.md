# Capgo Browser Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Capgo Browser ("the app") is made by Digital shift OU, operating Capgo ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## Summary

- We do not run servers for this app and we do not receive your data.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- Your ChatGPT sign in is stored only in your device Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).

## Data processed on your device

- **ChatGPT credentials**: access token, refresh token and ID token from OpenAI. Stored in the secure Keychain/Keystore. Never written to plain storage. Deleted when you sign out.
- **Email address**: read from your ChatGPT ID token to show which account is connected. Stays on device.
- **Browsing data**: pages, cookies and cache of the in-app browser stay on your device. Clear them anytime in Settings > "Close browser and clear session".
- **Preferences**: whether you finished the introduction.

## Data sent to OpenAI

When you use the agent, the app sends your messages, and the text, screenshots or elements of pages the agent reads, directly from your device to OpenAI's API using your own ChatGPT plan. Requests are sent with storage disabled (`store: false`). OpenAI processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

The app never sends this data to Capgo.

## Websites you visit

Websites you open in the app may collect data under their own privacy policies, like in any browser.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and requires a ChatGPT account, which has its own age requirements.

## Your choices

- Use the browser without signing in.
- Sign out at any time from Settings. This deletes all stored tokens.
- Delete the app to remove all local data.
- You can revoke the app's access from your ChatGPT account settings.
