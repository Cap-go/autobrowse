# Auto Browse Support

Need help? Email **tori@capgo.app**. We answer within 2 business days.

## FAQ

**Do I need a ChatGPT account?** Only for the AI agent. The browser works without signing in.

**Does it cost extra?** No. The agent uses your own ChatGPT plan limits.

**I hit a usage limit.** Your ChatGPT plan has usage limits. Wait for them to reset or upgrade your plan with OpenAI.

**How do I sign out or delete my data?** Settings (gear icon) > Sign out. "Close browser and clear session" removes cookies and cache. Deleting the app removes everything.

**Is my data sent to Capgo?** No. Requests go directly from your device to OpenAI.
